Title: RMIT Student Legal Service
Source: https://www.rmit.edu.au/students/support-services/financial-legal-support/legal-service
Verified: 2026-10-04

RMIT Student Legal Service provides free and confidential legal help to current RMIT students. The service can assist with a range of legal issues, including some tenancy, debt, fines, employment, family-law, intellectual-property and academic matters.

Students can submit an enquiry or book an appointment. If a student is unsure whether the issue is legal, RMIT suggests speaking with a Student Welfare Advisor first.

Use this source for questions about legal issues, tenancy disputes, fines, debt, employment rights or whether a student should contact the legal service.
