Title: RMIT Study Support
Source: https://www.rmit.edu.au/students/support-services/study-support
Verified: 2026-10-04

RMIT Study Support provides free academic-skills services and resources. Students can get help with understanding assignments, finding information, referencing, academic writing, study techniques, academic English, maths and science skills, and assignment planning.

One-to-one support is available through Academic Skills Advisors, Maths and Science Advisors and Librarians. Peer mentoring and online study resources are also available.

Use this source for study-skills questions, referencing, assignment planning, academic writing, research help and general academic-skills support. It is not a service that completes assessed work for students.
