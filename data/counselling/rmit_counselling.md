Title: RMIT Counselling and Psychological Services
Source: https://www.rmit.edu.au/students/support-services/health-safety-wellbeing/mental-health-counselling/counselling
Verified: 2026-10-04

RMIT Counselling and Psychological Services provides free, confidential and professional short-term counselling for students experiencing concerns such as anxiety, depression and stress. Students do not need to be in crisis to seek support.

The first appointment is an intake consultation where a counsellor discusses the student's concerns and identifies suitable options. Depending on need, options can include short-term RMIT counselling, groups or webinars, external counselling, self-guided resources, or referral to other RMIT services such as Welfare, Equitable Learning, Study Support, Safer Community or Student Legal.

Use this source for questions about counselling, emotional support, stress, anxiety, low mood, or whether a student needs to be in crisis before contacting counselling.
