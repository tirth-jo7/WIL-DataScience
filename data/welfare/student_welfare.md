Title: RMIT Student Welfare Advisors
Source: https://www.rmit.edu.au/students/support-services/student-welfare-advisors
Verified: 2026-10-04

Student Welfare Advisors provide free and confidential short-term assistance to students who are struggling to navigate university processes or support. They can help with finance, accommodation, urgent welfare needs, budgeting, debt management, Centrelink guidance, university processes and referrals to other RMIT or external services.

Welfare Advisors can also help students who feel overwhelmed and have several issues at once. They can guide students through processes such as special consideration, leave of absence, reduced study load, appeals and complaints, but they do not provide counselling or psychological treatment.

The service is available to eligible enrolled RMIT students, including domestic and international students.
