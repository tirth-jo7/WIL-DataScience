Title: RMIT extensions and special consideration
Source: https://www.rmit.edu.au/students/my-course/assessment-results/special-consideration-extensions
Verified: 2026-10-04

RMIT provides assessment adjustments including extensions and special consideration.

A short extension can be relevant when unforeseen short-term circumstances outside the student's control affect the ability to submit an assessment by the deadline. RMIT's extension page explains that extensions can apply to assessment tasks with deadlines, while timed assessments use other processes.

Special consideration may be available when unexpected circumstances beyond the student's control affect the ability to complete an assessment. Examples described by RMIT include short-term physical or mental ill-health, difficult personal circumstances, bereavement, serious disruption to living arrangements, sudden financial hardship and some other unexpected events.

Students with an ongoing disability, long-term illness or mental health condition may instead need Equitable Learning support and equitable assessment arrangements.

Use this source to explain the difference between extensions, special consideration and ongoing equitable adjustments. Do not guarantee eligibility or an outcome.
