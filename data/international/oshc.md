Title: RMIT Overseas Student Health Cover requirements
Source: https://www.rmit.edu.au/study-with-us/international-students/apply-to-rmit-international-students/student-visas/health-cover-requirements
Verified: 2026-10-04

International students on an Australian student visa generally need valid Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) for the duration required by their visa. OSHC is health insurance intended to help cover medical costs while studying in Australia.

RMIT can arrange Medibank Comprehensive OSHC when a student accepts an offer, or a student can arrange cover with another approved provider and provide the required details to RMIT. Coverage, waiting periods, provider rules and gap fees depend on the policy.

Use this source for questions about what OSHC is, why it is required, arranging OSHC through RMIT, or where to ask OSHC-related questions. Do not make claims about an individual's exact insurance coverage unless supported by retrieved policy information.
