Title: RMIT Student Connect
Source: https://www.rmit.edu.au/students/support-services/student-connect
Verified: 2026-10-04

Student Connect is RMIT's general contact point for current-student administration, support and academic enquiries. Topics include enrolment, courses and programs, fees and finance, results and graduation, timetables, appointments, wellbeing and housing, global-learning programs and other general enquiries.

Students can use the online portal to submit an enquiry. Other contact channels include webchat, phone and face-to-face service, with hours listed on the official Student Connect page.

Use this source when the student's question is administrative, general, or when no more specialised support service is clearly appropriate.
