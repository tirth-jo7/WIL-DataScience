Title: RMIT Emergency and crisis support
Source: https://www.rmit.edu.au/about/our-locations-and-facilities/facilities/safety-security/campus-security/emergency-crisis-support
Verified: 2026-10-04

RMIT publishes emergency and crisis contacts for students. In Australia, immediate emergencies should be directed to emergency services. RMIT also lists Campus Security and an urgent mental health support phone and text service. The page includes separate contacts for students outside Australia, including exchange students and international students located offshore.

Use this source for urgent or crisis-navigation questions. It should not be used as a substitute for routine counselling information.
