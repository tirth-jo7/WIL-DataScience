Title: RMIT Equitable Learning Services
Source: https://www.rmit.edu.au/students/support-services/equitable-learning
Verified: 2026-10-04

Equitable Learning Services supports eligible students with disability, long-term illness, mental health conditions, neurodivergence or certain primary caring responsibilities. The service works with students to develop an Equitable Learning Plan and reasonable adjustments that support participation in study.

Examples of support can include assessment adjustments, accessible course materials, assistive technology and other study-related adjustments depending on the student's needs and eligibility.

Short-term conditions may be better handled through extensions or special consideration. Equitable Learning does not replace counselling or general study-skills support.

Use this source for questions about ongoing study adjustments, disability or neurodiversity support, Equitable Learning Plans and the difference between ongoing adjustments and short-term assessment flexibility.
