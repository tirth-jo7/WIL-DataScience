Title: RMIT support for international students
Source: https://www.rmit.edu.au/students/support-services/international-students
Verified: 2026-10-04

RMIT provides dedicated information and support for international students. The international-student support hub links students to study and English-language support, career services, health and wellbeing services, social connection, inclusion services and other RMIT support.

International students can also use many general RMIT services, including counselling, Study Support, Student Connect and other student-support services, subject to the eligibility rules of each service.

Use this source for broad questions about support available to international students or where an international student can start when they are unsure which service to use.
