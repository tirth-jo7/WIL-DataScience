Title: RMIT financial and legal support
Source: https://www.rmit.edu.au/students/support-services/financial-legal-support
Verified: 2026-10-04

RMIT provides several types of support for students experiencing financial difficulty. Options include financial welfare advice, Student Hardship Assistance Grants, government and community financial-support information, and the RMIT Student Legal Service.

A Welfare Advisor can help students understand financial-assistance options, emergency accommodation, budgeting and referrals. Hardship assistance may be relevant for urgent essential living needs. Eligibility and the exact assistance available depend on the relevant program and the student's circumstances.

Use this source for broad questions about money problems, bills, groceries, rent, budgeting or where to start with financial support.
